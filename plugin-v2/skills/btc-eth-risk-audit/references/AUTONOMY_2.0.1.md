# V2.0.1 operational research loop

The full V2.0 policy text and its SHA stay unchanged. This release completes operational connections and does not claim a profitable new market model.

- A newly received official snapshot is copied to separate append-only research tables with its exact source SHA and actual server freeze time. The official signal/event tables are never written by this research path.
- Four independent prospective research directions are recorded. Missing structure, missing anchors, inadequate TP2 RR, already invalidated structure or a source older than one minute causes research abstention. This research freshness rule is not a new condition on an existing official pending signal.
- A research plan is never backfilled at a past price. Only complete subsequent one-minute paths can show a first touch and STOP/TP progression. Same-minute entry/exit ordering, conflicting bars and gaps remain unverified. Fees, adverse slippage and funding must be separately available before net performance is calculated.
- Opposing observed structures and frozen-structure breaks can create counterexample records before a terminal STOP. They are observations requiring review, not proven causal explanations. Mechanism revisions are generated as independent research candidates and cannot promote themselves.
- Every registered model has SHADOW, CANARY, LIVE or QUARANTINED exposure. SHADOW/QUARANTINED cannot be cited through modelId to authorize a new formal signal. A CANARY is limited to its explicit asset. Existing original formal signals and first-touch semantics are unchanged.
- A transition needs a new prospective phase, unused holdout, same model hash, real path/cost replay, adequate prelocked sample criteria/loss limits, actual current CI output and pinned operational evidence. Prior shadow evidence is not recycled as canary evidence.
- Consumed holdouts are committed before evaluation in the automatic job. Failed studies stay spent. Code rollback preserves the spent-holdout journal and never rolls back DB records.
- The deployment controller compares the actual running commit, policy hash, model-registry hash, absence of automatic orders, immutable original signal hashes and the existing event prefix. After failure it restores a previously verified source tree through a new commit, rejects concurrent-source overwrite and verifies the restored live response.

Keep these claims separate: implementation exists; technical tests passed; a live controlled rollback was observed; a model completed prospective shadow/canary; net market performance improved. Only report states actually observed. A live rollback drill does not prove a trading strategy profitable or imply complete coverage of all future failures.

Missing a prelocked independent study, actual baseline/cost/path data or sufficient future samples continues to block promotion. No promoted profitable model, all-market accuracy or hidden whale intention is implied. Supported symbolic condition graphs are the current self-revision language; this is not an unrestricted generator of arbitrary new algorithms.

Default user output remains the compact BTC/ETH table and the separate conditional outlook for each coin. Research internals are shown only when requested. No automatic exchange order permissions are enabled.
