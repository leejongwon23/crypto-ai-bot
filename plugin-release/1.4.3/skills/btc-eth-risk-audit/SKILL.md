## V1.9 operational repair — range-entry stop risk gate (2026-11-17)

This implements a **prospective** risk gate without changing the immutable V1.9 original prompt text, previously registered signals, or chronological STOP/TP events.

- Before proposing or registering **any new** `RANGE_BOUNDARY` formal signal, compare the proposed stop to the user-source **6h high/low** and independently check all four current 5m, 15m, 30m and 1h EMA alignments and both 24h/6h VWAPs. This is enforced by the deployed server function `evaluateRangeStopContext` within the V1.9 official submission validator. Do not merely label the trade as range-bound with a mathematically favorable narrow stop.
- If all four execution EMA alignments are `BULL`, observed price is above both VWAPs, and the proposed SHORT stop is at or below the source's 6h high, **reject that new range SHORT** (`RANGE_SHORT_STOP_INSIDE_UNCLEARED_6H_HIGH`). Conversely, if all four are `BEAR`, price is below both VWAPs, and proposed LONG stop is at or above the 6h low, **reject that range LONG** (`RANGE_LONG_STOP_INSIDE_UNCLEARED_6H_LOW`). For a range proposal missing necessary source values, mark it unqualified (`RANGE_COUNTERTREND_CONTEXT_MISSING`). Neither rule is a generic veto on independently qualified trend trades.
- Check the *actual* structural wider stop and compute TP2 risk/reward again. If the corrected risk makes TP2 RR insufficient, **HOLD** rather than shrinking the stop or moving TP2 to a fabricated target. A negative CVD while price holds/rises can be a possible seller absorption hypothesis, not proof of future downward price response. Distinguish confirmed source facts from this hypothesis.
- Counterexample audited: at ETH 2026-11-17 06:35:19, original SHORT entry 1823.63, stop 1828.91, 6h high 1836.88, 5m-1h BULL and spot above both VWAPs. This new prospective check rejects that original-shaped candidate. The **already issued** signal and its observed subsequent STOP remain valid historical ledger records, not retroactively cancelled or rewritten.
- Still inspect actual 1m chronology, alternative LONG, SHORT, near/far entry and no-trade independently; this gate fixes one proven approval gap but is not a claim of improved realized win rate. Unit tests are necessary but not a substitute for full market-regime backtests.
